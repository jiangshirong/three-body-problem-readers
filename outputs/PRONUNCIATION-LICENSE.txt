Modifications: selected book vocabulary and its dictionary headwords; retained
only IPA sound records explicitly tagged US or General-American; restructured
and deduplicated records; retained source spelling, IPA, tags and grammatical
distinctions. Original IPA is retained as rawPronunciation; display IPA is
normalized to learner dictionary conventions (learner-us-1). No spelling
prediction or AI-generated pronunciation is used. CMUdict fallback records
are separately identified and retain the accompanying BSD-2-Clause license.
This modified data subset is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. When sharing it,
retain attribution, source links, this notice, and the same license.

An IPA shared by several accents is retained only when its SAME sound record
explicitly includes US or General-American. An unrelated American recording,
an American sense tag, North-America alone, untagged IPA or a UK-only sound
does not qualify. Original source tags remain available for auditing.

No Wiktionary audio is bundled. Manually retrieved recordings remain subject
to the relevant provider's terms and recording-specific license. This notice
does not license the EPUB book, ECDICT data, or third-party service outputs.
